03 / SCIENTIFIC METHOD

Recovery, defined fairly

1

Establish drought
Root-zone wetness at or below its 1991–2020 seasonal 20th percentile for 30+ days.

2

Date each recovery
First sustained return of surface AND root zone to the same 40th percentile. Delay = root minus surface.

3

Audit the conclusion
Compare against a model-inherent surrogate null, test 9 threshold settings, and report INCONCLUSIVE when evidence is thin.

This tool investigates a gridded, model-derived environmental signal. It does not diagnose an individual farm or prove crop losses.
